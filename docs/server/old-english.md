# Old English Style Build Phases

[Old English Style](https://wolfcraft-mc.com/old-english-stylepack/) is
a medieval-inspired pack with multipurpose buildings that pack a lot of use
into its buildings. Each complex houses one or more worker huts, a residence,
and an auto-leveling guard tower. It’s relatively resource-light, making use
of common woods and stone (and lots of paper at level 5)

## Notes

* Generally, the order of assignment, when automatic assignment is set in the
    colony, is guardsman, then civilian job. This is not always the case, but
    I haven't found anything that explains why or under what conditions it
    happens.
* When a guardsman is drafted, a civilian slot/bed is opened. Conversely, if
    a guardsman is reverted to a civilian, a slot/bed is filled.
* All residences and most huts have an autoleveling guard tower. This means
    that when the building is being built, *as soon as the guard tower is
    placed*, a guardsman is drafted, even before the hut is finished.
* Assume a building or a complex has a residence and auto leveling guard
    towers. Missing or multiple residences and guard towers will be noted.

**IMPORTANT**: You will need to manually build and upgrade residences that are
included in a complex (such as the Forester and Miner).

* Scout location.
* Start a farm.
* Find and pen animals.
  * Cow
  * Sheep
  * Pig

  * Rabbit

## Phase 1 - Fundamentals

* Place Supply Camp/Ship
* Place Town Hall

  * Level as many times as you can
* Warehouse (no worker)
  * Two Guard Towers
  * Two Courier Huts
* Town Hall
  * Two Guard Towers
  * Tavern (no worker)
  * Restaurant

## Population – Phase 2

Target Colony Size: **12–18 total**

* Civilians: 8–14

  * 2 Builders
  * 1 Courier
  * 1 Cook
  * 1 Warehouse worker
  * 1 Farmer
  * 1 Forester
  * 1 Miner
  * Flex roles
* Guardsmen: 4 (baseline) + optional expansion if more towers added

Mechanics Notes:

* Any additional completed tower will immediately draft another guard.
* Guard count should not exceed equipment production capacity.
* Civilian-to-guard ratio should remain production-positive.

# Phase 3 – Defense Scaling (Intentional Tower Expansion)

**Goal:** Expand defense deliberately instead of passively.

Old English style embeds additional guard towers in:

* Residences
* Many production huts

Each completed tower will auto-draft immediately.

Critical Support Requirements:

* Blacksmith
* Armorer
* Stable weapon production
* Stable armor production
* Guard-related research

Do not allow tower count to exceed equipment throughput.

## Population – Phase 3

Target Colony Size: **18–30+ total**

* Civilians: 14–22
* Guardsmen: 6–10 (driven strictly by tower count)

Mechanics Notes:

* Guards free housing slots but increase food and gear demand.
* Population planning must account for automatic drafting behavior.
* Tower placement = immediate population role conversion.

---

# Strategic Notes

* Phase 1 begins with a forced 4-guard military baseline.
* Equipment production is not optional in this style — it is mandatory early.
* Treat additional towers as population triggers, not decorations.
* Embedded residences remain modular growth toggles.
* Builder queue planning is critical due to embedded hut components.
* Monitor sprawl if playing on a chunk-limited server.

---

# Summary Philosophy

This style creates:

* Immediate distributed defense
* Early military overhead
* Mixed-use civic clustering
* Fine-grained but mechanically constrained population control

Plan growth as a synchronization problem between:

* Builder throughput
* Production chains
* Equipment supply
* Food supply
* Automatic guard drafting behavior
